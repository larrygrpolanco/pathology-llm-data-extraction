# BACKGROUND:

Mutations in BRAF or RAS genes or RET/PTC rearrangements are found in more than 70% of papillary thyroid carcinomas. The BRAF V600E mutation has been associated with more aggressive behavior of papillary carcinoma. Mutations in the RAS genes or PAX8/PPARg rearrangement occur in approximately 70% of follicular thyroid carcinomas and with lower frequency in oncocytic (Hürthle cell) carcinomas. Regarding the specificity of these mutations for cancer, BRAF V600E mutation and RET/PTC rearrangements are overall specific for malignancy in the thyroid, although they are found in a very low frequency in benign thyroid lesions.

# MUTATIONAL ANALYSIS:

For paraffin-embedded surgical specimens, molecular analysis was performed to identify mutations of tumor cells. Specimens with the minimum of 50% tumor cellularity were accepted for the analysis. Optical density readings were obtained. Real-time PCR was performed on melting curve analysis to detect possible mutations. Single-step real-time PCR was performed to amplify BRAF codons 690-601, NRAS codon 81, HRAS codon 61, and KRAS codons 12113 sequences. If required, the mutation was coordinated by identifying the fusion points of the rearrangements.
