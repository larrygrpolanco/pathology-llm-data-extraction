# B.

The specimen is designated "thyroid" and consists of a 5.0 x 3.5 x 3.5 cm thyroid gland that weighs 17 grams. The outer surface is tan-purple smooth and glistening. The entire specimen is inked black and the specimen is serially sectioned to reveal a 3.9 x 2.7 x 2.0 cm well-circumscribed tan firm nodule present in the right thyroid lobe that grossly abuts the black inked capsular surface. The nodule is hemorrhagic over approximately 40%. The right lobe also displays a separate 0.5 x 0.3 x 0.3 cm tan-white well-circumscribed nodule which is located 1.0 cm from the largest nodule. The left lobe of the thyroid contains a 0.2 x 0.2 x 0.2 cm tan-white well-circumscribed nodule that grossly abuts the black inked capsular surface. This nodule is located 2.4 cm from the largest nodules. The remaining thyroid parenchyma is dark red and rubbery with no other discrete lesions identified. A representative section of the largest nodule is submitted for frozen section and frozen section cassette BF1 is resubmitted as received.
