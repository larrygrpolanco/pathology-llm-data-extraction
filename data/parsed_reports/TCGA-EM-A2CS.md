This tumor replaces the entire thyroid gland in an infiltrative pattern. The diffuse nature of this tumor is reminiscent of the diffuse sclerosing variant, but in this case the abundant fibrosis, psammoma bodies and chronic inflammation that characterize that variant are not seen. The tumor extends to the painted margins of resection in right and left lobes and in the isthmus.
