According to this orientation, the specimen is 2.2 cm from 3 o'clock to 9 o'clock, 1.0 cm from 12 o'clock to 6 o'clock, and is 0.2 cm thick.

The margins are inked as follows: 12 o'clock half blue, 6 o'clock half yellow, 3 o'clock tip orange, 9 o'clock tip green. The specimen is serially sectioned from 3 o'clock to 9 o'clock. # B.

Received fresh labeled "total thyroid" is a total thyroidectomy specimen weighing 34 grams. The right lobe is 5.0 x 4.0 x 2.0 cm, and the left lobe is 5.4 x 2.8 x 1.8 cm, and the isthmus is 1.7 x 1.5 x 0.3 cm. The right lobe is inked blue, the left lobe is inked black, and the isthmus is inked yellow.

Sectioning demonstrates a suspicious tan nodule in the right superior pole measuring 2.0 cm in greatest dimension. The nodule is encapsulated, is mostly red-brown, with a central area of firm, tan-gray, suspicious tissue. In the mid portion to inferior right lobe, there is a well-circumscribed, diffusely hemorrhagic, pink-tan nodule measuring 1.8 cm in greatest dimension, which is not suspicious. The remainder of the parenchyma is dark red and grossly unremarkable. The isthmus and left thyroid lobe are grossly unremarkable.
