# GROSS DESCRIPTION

(A) TOTAL THYROIDECTOMY - An asymmetric thyroid. The left lobe (4.5 x 3.0 x 2.0 cm) is distended by a central well-circumscribed soft pink-tan mass (2.2 x 1.7 x 1.5 cm) located in the central portion of the left lobe). Two additional nodules are present in the lower portion of the lobe, a 0.6 cm well-circumscribed more laterally and a firm nodule (1.0 x 0.8 x 0.8 cm in the inferior aspect of the lobe). This lower most firm nodule shows papillary projections on cut surface and is confined to the thyroid.

The isthmus (1.5 x 0.5 x 0.3 cm) is unremarkable. The right lobe (5.0 x 2.0 x 1.0 cm) is also unremarkable. A well-circumscribed nodule is noted in the right inferior location (0.5 x 0.3 x 0.3 cm).
