The specimens are received in two properly labeled containers with the patient's name and accession number, two frozen sections are performed.

The specimen is designated "total thyroid" and consists of a 51.4 gram total thyroidectomy specimen, with the right lobe measuring 5.5 x 4.5 x 3.5 cm, and the left lobe measuring 4.0 x 2.0 x 1.0 cm. The external surface is smooth and tan-brown. The specimen is inked as follows: right lobe-black, left lobe-blue, isthmus yellow. The remaining thyroid parenchyma is red-brown and meaty. Sectioning of the isthmus and left lobe reveals unremarkable parenchyma. The mass does not appear to invade the capsule.
